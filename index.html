Three Pillars of Senior Health

The body tells the truth.
Are you listening?

🔥

Visceral Fat Is the Silent Risk

The fat surrounding your internal organs silently raises risk of heart disease, type 2 diabetes, and metabolic syndrome — invisible on the scale, but precisely revealed by Tanita BIA.

💪

Muscle Is the Organ of Longevity

After 60, sarcopenia — age-related muscle loss — threatens independence. Muscle regulates blood sugar, supports bone density, and prevents falls. Resistance training is your most powerful medicine.

🦴

Bone Density Protects Your Future

Bone density peaks at 30 and declines silently after 40. Weight-bearing exercise, calcium, and daily sunlight are the three pillars. Know your baseline — protect your mobility for life.
