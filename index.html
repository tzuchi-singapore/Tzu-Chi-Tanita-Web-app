The Science of Longevity

Health is not just about weight.
It's about what you're made of.

Body composition — the balance of muscle, fat, bone, and water — is one of the most powerful predictors of healthy ageing. Understanding yours is the first step to transforming it.

🔥

Visceral Fat Is the Silent Risk

Excess fat surrounding your internal organs silently raises your risk of heart disease, type 2 diabetes, and metabolic syndrome — invisible on the scale, but revealed by the Tanita BIA.

💪

Muscle Is the Organ of Longevity

After 60, sarcopenia — age-related muscle loss — becomes one of the greatest threats to independence. Muscle regulates blood sugar, supports bone density, and prevents falls. Resistance training is your most powerful medicine.

🦴

Bone Density Protects Your Future

Bone density peaks at 30 and declines silently after 40. Weight-bearing exercise, adequate calcium, and daily sunlight are the three pillars of bone health. Know your baseline — protect your mobility for life.
