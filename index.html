The Science of Longevity

Your health is not just about weight.
It's about what you're made of.

Body composition — the balance of muscle, fat, bone, and water — is one of the most powerful predictors of healthy ageing. Understanding yours is the first step to changing it.

🔥

Fat Does Not Define You — But It Affects You

Excess visceral fat — the fat surrounding your internal organs — silently raises your risk of cardiovascular disease, type 2 diabetes, and metabolic syndrome. It is invisible on the scale, but the Tanita BIA reveals it precisely.

💪

Muscle Is the Organ of Longevity

Muscle is not just for appearance. It regulates glucose, supports bone density, prevents falls, and keeps your metabolism strong. After 60, sarcopenia — muscle loss — becomes one of the greatest threats to independence. Resistance training is your most powerful antidote.

🦴

Strong Bones Mean Years of Mobility

Bone density peaks in your 30s and silently declines after 40. Weight-bearing exercise, adequate calcium, and daily sunlight (Vitamin D) are the three pillars of bone health. Know your baseline — so you can protect it.
